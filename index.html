About

A useful check—not a safety guarantee

What it does

RecallCheck identifies a product through community-maintained Open Food Facts data, then compares it with locally cached FDA and USDA recall information. Government notices are authoritative.

Why the package matters

A barcode may identify a product line, while only specific lots or dates are recalled. Check codes printed near the seal, nutrition panel, package bottom, or cap.

Privacy

Camera video is processed locally and is neither uploaded nor saved. Barcodes remain only for this browser session. There are no accounts, cookies, ads, or analytics.

Limitations

Coverage and product records may be incomplete or delayed. This tool does not assess food safety, diagnose illness, or replace an official notice.

RecallCheck is an experimental prototype and does not replace official FDA, USDA, manufacturer, retailer, or healthcare guidance. Recall information can change, and some recalls may not include a barcode.
