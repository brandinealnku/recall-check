About and limitations

A useful check—not a safety guarantee

What RecallCheck checks

RecallCheck uses Open Food Facts to identify a product, then compares it with locally cached FDA and USDA recall information. Official notices are authoritative.

Your privacy

Camera video is processed on this device and is never uploaded or saved. Barcodes are not persisted. There are no accounts, cookies, ads, or analytics.

Coverage and product records may be incomplete or delayed. RecallCheck does not assess food safety, diagnose illness, or replace an official notice.
