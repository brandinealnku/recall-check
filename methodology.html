RecallCheck methodology

RecallCheck is an independent ITSBAD LLC product. Official notices remain authoritative.

Sources and refresh

Locally cached openFDA food-enforcement and USDA FSIS recall records are refreshed by the documented automated workflow. The interface reports the dataset timestamp and partial agency coverage.

Matching

Exact UPC and GTIN identifiers are evaluated first. Equivalent GTIN matching accounts for valid zero-padded representations. Product information comes from Open Food Facts. Similar names, brands, or packages are context only and never confirmation.

Current and historical

Official lifecycle fields distinguish active records from closed or terminated records. Some notices contain no barcode, so package lot, date, establishment, and size details may be essential.

Boundaries and limitations

RecallCheck does not determine safety, illness, expiration, or whether a specific package is affected when required details are absent. Data may be delayed or incomplete. Partial coverage means one agency could not be checked.

Privacy

Checks are not persisted. Camera images are not uploaded. Session history uses generic labels in JavaScript memory only. There are no accounts, cookies, analytics, fingerprinting, or behavioral tracking.
