Privacy

RecallCheck has no accounts, cookies, analytics, advertising trackers, or permanent scan history. Camera frames stay on the device. Barcodes and package codes are not stored or placed in URLs. Optional feedback excludes product and package identifiers automatically. Session history is memory-only and disappears on reload.
