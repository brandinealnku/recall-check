Privacy

Designed to minimize what leaves your device.

RecallCheck does not require an account and does not use advertising trackers. Some product-identification requests use Open Food Facts, while recall records are served by RecallCheck for comparison in your browser.

Camera and barcode scanning

Camera frames used to scan a barcode stay on your device and are not uploaded by RecallCheck. A scanned or manually entered barcode may be sent to Open Food Facts to identify the product. RecallCheck then compares the barcode and related product information with its available FDA and USDA recall dataset.

Recent checks on this device

RecallCheck can save up to six recent checks in your browser's local storage so you can quickly check a product again. Those records stay in this browser on this device and are not sent to ITSBAD Labs. You can clear them from the Recent checks section.

Recent-check entries may include the product name, barcode, result label, result heading, and the time of the check. Clearing browser storage can also remove them.

Feedback

When local feedback controls are available, RecallCheck can save a limited feedback record in this browser. The current implementation stores feedback locally rather than sending it to ITSBAD Labs. Product and package identifiers are not intentionally transmitted through this feedback feature.

Third-party services

Product identification may use Open Food Facts. Links to official recall notices may take you to FDA or USDA websites. Those services have their own privacy practices and policies.

No account required.

RecallCheck is intentionally usable without creating a profile, signing in, or providing your name or email address.
